**Description (4000):**

```
Covenant Household is a Reformed daily prayer book for family worship.

"As for me and my house, we will serve the LORD." (Joshua 24:15)

Each day has morning worship and evening worship:

• A psalm to sing, from the Scottish Psalter of 1650
• A lesson from the Old Testament and a lesson from the New Testament
• On Sundays and feasts, the Epistle and the Gospel
• The collect of the week, from the Book of Common Prayer of 1662

For the household, each day also has a proverb and a part of the Westminster Standards or the Canons of Dort. Some days have a writing by J. C. Ryle on the Gospel.

THE CHURCH YEAR
The readings follow the calendar of the Book of Common Prayer and the Revised Tables of Lessons of 1922. Where the table reads the Apocrypha, a canonical lesson takes its place (Westminster Confession 1.3).

YOUR BIBLE
Read in the King James Version, the Berean Standard Bible, or the Dutch Statenvertaling. With the Statenvertaling, sing the Dutch psalter of 1773.

WITH THE OTHER APPS
• Sing the psalm in Precentor, with the tune in four parts
• Read each passage with the notes of the Self-Interpreting Bible
• Memorise a verse or a catechism answer in Hearers

FREE FOREVER
"Freely ye have received, freely give." (Matthew 10:8)
Covenant Household is free. It has no account, no advertisements and no tracking. The readings are in the app, so it works with no connection.
```

**Notes for the reviewer:**

```
No account is needed. Open the app, choose a language and a Bible, and the readings for today show. All readings are in the app and work offline. Links open the sister apps (Precentor, Hearers, the Self-Interpreting Bible) in the browser.
```
